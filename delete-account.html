Delete Your Trailmark Account

This permanently deletes your Trailmark account and the data tied to it. Specifically:

  • Deleted: your account/login, your private backup of My Content (trails, waypoints), your saved vehicles and maintenance records, and your uploaded photos.
  • Anonymized, not deleted: anything you shared with a crew group — shared waypoints, trails, photos, and chat messages stay visible to that crew, just no longer attributed to you.

This can't be undone.
