Trailmark Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Trailmark is an offline-first trail mapping and navigation application. This policy explains what information Trailmark handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information Trailmark handles

  • Location: precise location, direction, speed, altitude, and route history may be used to show your position, record rides, navigate trails, tag waypoints or photos, and support navigation while the app is in the background.
  • Account information: if you sign in, Trailmark processes your email address, display name, authentication credentials, and account identifier through its authentication provider.
  • User content: trails, waypoints, photos, group membership, messages, and live crew locations are processed when you choose to create, save, back up, or share them.
  • Local app data: downloaded maps, saved routes, settings, and locally recorded content are stored on your device so Trailmark can work offline.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide Trailmark features, including maps, weather and elevation lookups, route recording and navigation, offline access, optional backups, and optional crew sharing. Trailmark does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Sharing and service providers

When online features are used, relevant requests may be sent to service providers that operate Trailmark's authentication and data storage, map and imagery tiles, weather, radar, elevation, and cell-coverage information. Crew content and live location are visible to other members of the crew group you join. Public community trails are visible to Trailmark users. Providers may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of normal network communication.

Retention and control

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Online content remains until it is deleted or the associated account is removed, subject to limited backup retention by service providers. You can use Trailmark without signing in, stop location access in your device settings, leave crew sharing, delete local content, and sign out at any time.

Security

Trailmark uses encrypted network connections and access controls intended to protect online information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children

Trailmark is not directed to children under 13, and it is not knowingly used to collect personal information from children under 13.

Requests and contact

To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of online account data, open a support request in the Trailmark support repository. Do not include passwords, precise location history, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Trailmark changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
